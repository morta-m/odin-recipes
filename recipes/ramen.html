Classic Ramen

Ramen is a popular Japanese noodle soup that comes in various flavors and styles. This recipe provides a simple yet flavorful version of classic ramen that you can make at home.

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 packs of ramen noodles
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon miso paste
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 soft-boiled egg (optional)
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup baby spinach

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, heat the sesame oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger, and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken or vegetable broth, soy sauce, and miso paste to the pot. Stir well to combine and bring the mixture to a simmer.
  3. Add the sliced mushrooms to the broth and let them cook for about 5 minutes until they are tender.
  4. While the broth is simmering, cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  5. Add the baby spinach to the broth and let it wilt for about 1-2 minutes.
